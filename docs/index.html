GEM fit is a transparent rule, not a model output: growth board (35), small deal (30), emerging-market exchange (20), capital-hungry sector (15). Unknown inputs score half, and are labelled. Open any row to see the points.

Emails carry their evidence. Verified = the person's own address, published by the company on its site or in a regulatory filing. Inferred = the naming pattern of another officer's published address, applied to a confirmed name. Guessed = not published: Hunter.io's usual pattern for the domain applied to a confirmed name, with Hunter's mailbox check shown; guesses the check rejects are dropped. Company inboxes and the investor-relations contact named in the filing are shown separately. Otherwise N/A.

Thai and Chinese text keeps its original script, with English in parentheses: names are romanised (the person's own spelling may differ) and quotes are translated, both by Claude. Translations are for reading only; nothing is matched or scored on them.

Sector, description and missing officers are extracted by a model from the linked documents, and kept only if the supporting quote is found verbatim in the source. Those quotes are shown in each row.
